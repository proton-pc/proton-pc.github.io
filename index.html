What Proton VPN is

Proton VPN is a VPN service from Proton AG, the Geneva company that also makes an encrypted email service. It encrypts the traffic between your device and its servers, hides your IP address from the sites you visit and, according to the company, keeps no activity logs. The apps are open source and cover Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, Chromebook, two browsers and three kinds of TV.

There are three ways in. The free plan costs nothing and has no data cap, but it covers one device and picks a server in one of 10 countries for you. VPN Plus unlocks the full network of 20,000+ servers in 140+ countries, streaming, P2P and the NetShield blocker on 10 devices. The Unlimited bundle adds the paid versions of the company's mail, storage, password and calendar apps. The free vs Plus comparison shows the exact line between the tiers, and the pricing page lists every term with its renewal price.

Frequently asked questions

What is Proton VPN?

It is a VPN service from Proton AG in Switzerland, the maker of an encrypted email service. It encrypts your connection, hides your IP address and offers a free plan next to the paid VPN Plus and Unlimited plans.

Is it free?

Yes. There is a free plan with no data cap and no ads, limited to one device and servers in 10 countries chosen at random. Paid plans start at $2.99 a month on the current one year offer.

How many servers does it have?

Paid plans give access to more than 20,000 servers in over 140 countries, with speeds of up to 10 Gbps. The free plan uses servers in 10 countries that the app selects.

Does it keep logs?

The company states that it keeps no logs that could identify your activity and that Swiss law cannot force it to start. Its apps are open source and independently audited, with the results published.
