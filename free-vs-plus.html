What both plans share

The free tier is not a cut down trial. It uses the same open source apps, the same no logs policy and the same protocols as the paid plans. The kill switch is on every plan, Stealth is open to free users for getting past VPN blocks, and the Windows app lists DNS and IPv6 leak protection and alternative routing as free features. There is no cap on data, and the company says the free plan carries no ads.

That covers the most common reason to use a VPN at all: protecting traffic on public WiFi. One laptop on a café network is exactly what the free plan is built for, and the company says paying users fund it so that free users are not the product.

Where the free plan stops

The limits are about choice and scale. You get one connection at a time, so a phone and a laptop cannot both be protected at once. The app connects you to a server in one of 10 countries it selects, which is fine for privacy but no use if you need a particular location for a bank or a news site. The plan table lists free speed as medium.

Streaming, P2P and NetShield are missing entirely. On Windows the paid labels also cover Secure Core, split tunneling, VPN Accelerator and streaming. If any one of those is the reason you want a VPN, the free plan will not do the job. Which apps each device needs is set out on the devices and requirements page.

Who should stay on Free, and who should pay

Costs and renewal terms for each paid term are on the pricing page, and the review gives an overall score.

  • Stay on Free if you protect one device on public networks and do not mind which country you appear in.
  • Stay on Free if you want to try the apps before paying anything.
  • Pay for Plus if you need a chosen country, streaming or torrents.
  • Pay for Plus if a phone, a laptop and a TV all need cover at the same time.
  • Look at the Unlimited bundle only if you will also use the paid mail, storage or password apps.

Is there a free trial of Plus?

The company does not offer a time limited free trial of VPN Plus. The free plan plays that role, and paid plans come with a 30 day money back guarantee, so you can pay for Plus and ask for a refund within 30 days if it does not suit you. At times the pricing page also offers the first month of Plus for $1.00, renewing at $9.99 a month.

Both plans run in the same apps, and the plan is tied to your account rather than to a separate installer. You can get the apps from the download page.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free version of Proton VPN safe?

It uses the same apps, protocols and no logs policy as the paid plans, and it keeps the kill switch. The company says it can offer the free plan without ads because paying users fund the service.

Does the free plan have a data limit?

No. The company states there is no data limit on any plan, the free plan included. The limits are on devices, countries and features instead.

Can I choose a country on the free plan?

No. Free users connect to servers in 10 countries that are selected at random. Choosing a specific country needs VPN Plus or the Unlimited bundle.

Can I watch Netflix with the free plan?

No. Streaming is listed as not included on the free plan. It is part of VPN Plus and the Unlimited bundle.

How many devices can I use for free?

One device at a time. VPN Plus and Unlimited allow 10 at once, and a router counts as one connection however many devices sit behind it.
