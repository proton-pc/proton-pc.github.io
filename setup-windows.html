Before you start

Setting up Proton VPN on Windows takes three things.

  • An account: free, VPN Plus or Unlimited. People who already use the company's email service have one.
  • Your PC's architecture: x64 for most desktops and laptops, ARM64 for the Surface Pro, Surface Laptop, Galaxy Book S and similar ARM machines.
  • The installer from protonvpn.com; the download page of this guide explains which file is which.

Install and connect

  1. Create an accountSign up for the free plan or buy VPN Plus on protonvpn.com. A paid plan unlocks 10 devices and the full server list.
  2. Check x64 or ARM64Press Win + R, type msinfo32 and press Enter. In System Information, open System Summary and read System Type.
  3. Download the matching buildGet the x64 or ARM64 installer from the official Windows download page.
  4. Run the installerDouble click the downloaded file and follow the setup wizard to the end.
  5. Sign inOpen the app and log in with your username and password.
  6. Click Quick ConnectThe app connects to the fastest server for your location. Paid users can instead pick a country or a specialised server, such as a Tor server.

Settings worth turning on

Once connected, websites see the VPN server's address and your internet provider sees only encrypted traffic. A few settings are worth checking in the app.

  • Kill switch: blocks all traffic if the VPN drops; use it per session or as a permanent kill switch. Available on every plan.
  • NetShield: blocks malware domains, and on paid plans ads and trackers too.
  • Split tunneling: keeps chosen apps or IP addresses outside the tunnel. Paid plans.
  • Secure Core: routes traffic through a first server in Switzerland, Iceland or Sweden. Paid plans.
  • Protocol: Smart Protocol chooses automatically; Stealth helps where VPNs are blocked.

Not sure which of these your plan includes? The free vs Plus comparison lists them.

If it will not connect

The setup guide names three usual causes when a connection fails: the server is down for maintenance, the PC's clock does not match the time on the server's SSL certificate, or the network you are on restricts VPN traffic. Work through them in this order.

  1. Try a different server, since the first one may be under maintenance.
  2. Correct the date and time on the PC, then reconnect.
  3. On a restrictive network, switch the protocol to Stealth, or let alternative routing try to reach the servers through third party networks.
  4. If it still fails, contact the support team; paid plans get live chat most hours.

If it is slow

A VPN cannot raise the speed of the line underneath it. The company's advice is to connect to a server closer to you and to one with a lower load; the app shows the load next to each server name. On paid plans VPN Accelerator is available for long distance connections, and the 10 Gbps servers are reserved for VPN Plus and Unlimited.

If you also want the VPN on a phone or a TV, the devices page shows which app each one needs. On the free plan, remember that only one device can be connected at a time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I install Proton VPN on Windows?

Download the x64 or ARM64 installer from protonvpn.com, double click it and follow the wizard. Then sign in and click Quick Connect.

Is my PC x64 or ARM64?

Press Win + R, type msinfo32 and press Enter. System Summary shows the System Type; ARM machines include the Surface Pro, Surface Laptop and Samsung Galaxy Book S.

Can I use my existing email account login?

Yes. People who already use the company's email service have a VPN account and sign in to the app with the same username and password.

Why does the app fail to connect?

Common causes are a server under maintenance, a wrong system clock, or a network that blocks VPN traffic. Switch server, fix the clock, or try the Stealth protocol.

Does the VPN protect apps other than the browser?

Yes. The full Windows app routes all of the PC's internet traffic through the tunnel. Only the browser extensions are limited to the browser.
