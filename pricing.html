Proton VPN pricing has three tiers. The free plan costs nothing; VPN Plus is $9.99 for one month or $35.88 for the first year ($2.99 a month) and then renews at $83.88 a year; the Unlimited bundle is $12.99 a month or $77.88 for the first year, renewing at $119.88. Every paid plan has a 30 day money back guarantee.

What you pay in year two

The headline monthly figure is a first year price. On the current offer, VPN Plus bills $35.88 for the first 12 months and then $83.88 every 12 months, so year two costs $48 more than year one. The Unlimited bundle goes from $77.88 to $119.88. The homepage presents the current cut as up to 70% off for Black Friday, so the first year figure belongs to that sale.

Over two years on the yearly offer, VPN Plus comes to $119.76 ($35.88 plus one renewal at $83.88), against $239.76 for 24 single months at $9.99. The yearly term is the better deal even after renewal, as long as you know the second bill is coming.

What each plan includes

If you are not sure whether to pay at all, the free vs Plus page walks through the gap feature by feature.

  • Free: 1 device, medium speed, servers in 10 countries picked at random, no logs, no ads, kill switch and Stealth. No streaming, P2P or NetShield.
  • VPN Plus: 10 devices at once, the highest speed tier, 20,000+ servers in 140+ countries, streaming, P2P, the NetShield ad and malware blocker, Secure Core, priority support and live chat.
  • VPN Plus also includes the free tiers of the company's mail, storage and password apps, with limited calendar access.
  • Unlimited: everything in VPN Plus plus the paid mail, storage, password and calendar plans, including 500 GB of storage, 15 email addresses and 50 password vaults.

Discounts, coupons and the $1 first month

No coupon codes appear on the official pricing page. The discounts it shows are built into the terms: the Black Friday one year price, and an FAQ that quotes up to 65% off the monthly price for two years of VPN Plus, 55% for one year, and up to 38% for two years of Unlimited. Those FAQ figures describe the standard term discounts and sit apart from the current sale.

Choosing the free plan on the pricing page can open a popup that offers VPN Plus for $1.00 for the first month, renewing at $9.99 a month, with the option to cancel at any time. The site footer also links to a student discount whose terms sit on a separate page that this guide did not read.

Payment methods and plan changes

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Proton credits are accepted. Once you have an account you can buy credits with cash, a bank transfer or Bitcoin. You can switch term or plan at any time and the company applies prorated credit; moving down from Unlimited to VPN Plus means trimming email addresses, calendars or storage to fit the smaller plan.

Cancelling and refunds are covered on the refund and cancel page. Before you commit to any tier, the review sums up what the money buys, and the alternatives page lists the rivals the company compares itself with.

Frequently asked questions

How much is Proton VPN a month?

VPN Plus costs $9.99 for a single month. On a one year term it is $2.99 a month during the current offer, billed as $35.88 up front, and it renews at $83.88 a year. Prices were read on protonvpn.com on 26 November 2026.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 and covers one device with servers in 10 countries chosen at random. It has no data cap and no ads, but no streaming or P2P.

What happens when the first year ends?

The plan renews automatically at the regular yearly price: $83.88 for VPN Plus and $119.88 for the Unlimited bundle. Cancel before the renewal date if you do not want to pay that amount.

Can I pay with crypto or cash?

Not directly at checkout. You pay by card, PayPal or Proton credits, and after creating an account you can buy credits with cash, a bank transfer or Bitcoin.

Is the Unlimited bundle worth it over VPN Plus?

Only if you will use the other apps. Unlimited adds paid mail, 500 GB of storage, a password manager and calendar for $77.88 in the first year, against $35.88 for VPN Plus. The VPN part is the same in both.
