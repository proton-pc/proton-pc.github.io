What you get

When you download Proton VPN you get the full app, not a separate trial build. The same installer serves free and paying users, and what you can do depends on the plan tied to your account. The full apps send all of the device's traffic through the encrypted tunnel, while the Chrome and Firefox extensions protect only the browser.

The Windows app has a native ARM version for machines such as the Surface Laptop and Surface Pro. Android users can also get the app from F-Droid or as an APK from GitHub. For a walkthrough with the app's own button names, see the Windows setup guide.

Install steps

  1. Create an accountSign up for the free plan or buy VPN Plus on protonvpn.com. If you already use the company's email service, that account works too.
  2. Pick the right buildOn Windows, press Win + R, type msinfo32 and look at System Summary > System Type to see whether you need x64 or ARM64.
  3. InstallRun the downloaded file and follow the setup wizard. On phones and TVs, install from the App Store or Google Play instead.
  4. Sign inOpen the app and log in with your Proton username and password.
  5. ConnectClick Quick Connect to join the fastest server for your location, or pick a server your plan allows.

Free plan, paid plans and refund

The free plan needs no payment details and has no time limit. VPN Plus and the Unlimited bundle are subscriptions that renew automatically; on 26 November 2026 VPN Plus cost $9.99 a month or $35.88 for the first year. Every paid subscription has a 30 day money back guarantee, and the full rules are in the company's terms.

Frequently asked questions

Is Proton VPN free to download?

Yes. All the apps are free to download, and the free plan costs nothing to use on one device. Paid plans unlock more devices and features.

Which Windows version should I download?

Pick x64 for most PCs and ARM64 for ARM laptops such as the Surface Pro. If you are not sure, open System Information and check System Type under System Summary.

How big is the installer?

The company does not publish the installer size or a current version number on its download page. Your browser shows the file size once the download starts.

Do I need a separate account for each device?

No. One account signs in on every app. VPN Plus allows 10 devices connected at the same time, and the free plan allows one.

Is it safe to download from other sites?

Use protonvpn.com, the official app stores, F-Droid or the company's GitHub page. Copies from other sites can be altered and are not supported by the company.
