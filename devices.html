Proton VPN runs on Windows (x64 and ARM64), macOS, Linux, Android, iOS and iPadOS, Chromebook, Chrome and Firefox, Android TV, Apple TV and Fire TV. On Linux it is officially supported on Ubuntu and Debian with GNOME. The free plan covers one device at a time and paid plans cover 10, with a router counting as one.

Windows: x64 or ARM64

Windows comes in two builds. Most PCs need x64; ARM laptops such as the Microsoft Surface Laptop and Surface Pro, the Samsung Galaxy Book S, Lenovo Flex 5G, HP Elite Folio and Acer Spin 7 need the ARM64 build, which the company says has the same advanced features as the regular app. To check, open System Information with msinfo32 and read System Type. The Windows setup guide walks through the full install.

Linux, Mac and mobile

On Linux the company officially supports Ubuntu and Debian running the GNOME desktop. Other distributions may work, but support for them may be limited; the support centre has separate guides for Arch Linux and for the Linux GUI app.

Mac users install the macOS app from the official site, and iPhone and iPad users get the iOS and iPadOS app from the App Store. On Android the app is on Google Play, and the same open source app is available on F-Droid or as an APK from GitHub.

TVs and streaming sticks

The Android TV app installs from the TV's own app store, or from a desktop browser through Google Play by choosing the TV as the target device. The Apple TV app comes from the App Store on the device. Both ask you to sign in once through a browser, and both use the remote to pick a country, with a Fastest country option on Apple TV.

Fire TV is split. Amazon began moving newer Fire TV Sticks to Vega OS, a Linux based system, in late 2025. Older sticks on Fire OS use the Android app, while newer Vega OS sticks need the separate Vega OS app. Streaming on any TV app needs VPN Plus or the Unlimited bundle; the free vs Plus page has the details.

Device limits and routers

VPN Plus and the Unlimited bundle allow 10 devices or browsers connected at the same time; the free plan allows one. A router set up with the VPN protects every device behind it and counts as a single connection, which helps a household with more screens than the limit. The full apps protect the whole device, while the browser extensions cover only the browser.

When you know which app you need, the download page links to each one through the official site.

Frequently asked questions

Does Proton VPN work on Linux?

Yes. It is officially supported on Ubuntu and Debian with the GNOME desktop. Other distributions may work, but with limited support.

Does it work on a Fire TV Stick?

Yes. Use the Android app on Fire OS devices and the Vega OS app on newer sticks that run Vega OS. The support page explains how to tell which system your stick runs.

Is there an app for ARM Windows laptops?

Yes. A native ARM64 build covers devices such as the Surface Pro, Surface Laptop and Galaxy Book S, with the same features as the x64 app.

How many devices can I connect?

One on the free plan and 10 on VPN Plus or the Unlimited bundle. A router counts as one connection however many devices use it.

What are the minimum system requirements?

The download pages do not list minimum OS versions, memory or disk space. Check the release notes on the official site if your system is old.
